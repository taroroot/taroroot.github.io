Uma Up — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026 · Last updated: October 20, 2026

The short version: Uma Up only uses your Discord ID and display name so you can race with the people in your Activity. Race data lives in the server's memory and disappears when the race room empties. We have no database, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and we never sell or share your data.

This policy explains what information Uma Up (the "game"), a free game made by an independent developer ("we", "us"), uses when you play, and why. It should be read with our Terms of Service.

We don't ask for your email address, real name, friends list, messages, voice, avatar, location or payment details, and we don't read your Discord messages or voice chat. We don't use cookies, advertising, analytics or tracking pixels.

2. How race rooms work

When you join an online race, a small relay server passes game messages between the players in your Activity. One player's game (the "host") runs the race and sends each player only what they're allowed to see. The relay server keeps rooms in temporary memory only. A room, and everything in it, is deleted as soon as its last player leaves or the server restarts. We keep no race history.

Computer-controlled "(BOT)" players run inside the host's game. They don't send your data anywhere.

3. Who else is involved

  • Discord runs the platform the Activity lives on, and handles your Discord account and sign-in under Discord's Privacy Policy.
  • Hosting and network providers (for example, the company that hosts the game server or routes traffic to it) process connection data only to deliver the service, under their own privacy policies.
  • Other players in your race see your display name and your public game activity.

We don't sell, rent or share your information with anyone else. We'll only disclose information if the law requires it. Since we hold almost nothing, there is usually nothing to disclose.

4. Children

Uma Up is not meant for children under 13, or under the minimum age to use Discord where you live. We don't knowingly collect information from them. If you think a child has used the game, contact us and we'll help. Since we keep no stored player data, there is normally nothing to delete.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Stop sharing: close the Activity. You can also remove Uma Up's access to your account in Discord under User Settings → Authorized Apps.
  • Settings: clear your browser or Discord app storage to delete your saved settings.
  • Access, correction, deletion and other rights: depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have rights over your personal data. We don't keep stored player data, so in practice there is nothing for us to hand over or delete after your race ends. You're still welcome to contact us with any request, and we'll answer it.

6. Security

Connections to the game use encrypted HTTPS/WSS. The relay server checks your Discord identity before letting you into a room, and other players never receive your hidden votes. No system is perfectly secure, but keeping almost no data is the strongest protection we can offer.

7. International players

The game server may be in a different country from you. By playing, you understand that the limited, temporary data described above is processed there for the purposes in this policy.

8. Changes

If the game starts using more information, for example accounts or saved stats, we'll update this policy before that happens and change the "Last updated" date above.
